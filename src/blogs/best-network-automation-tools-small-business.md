# Best Network Automation Tools for Small Businesses (Without Enterprise Complexity)

Are you a small business owner or IT manager tired of manual network tasks, but overwhelmed by enterprise-grade automation platforms? You’re not alone. The good news: you don’t need a massive budget or a team of engineers to automate your network and boost reliability.

![Small business IT team reviewing their network setup](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1522071820081-009f0129c71c?w=1200&h=600&fit=crop)

## Why Small Businesses Need Network Automation

- **Save time** on repetitive tasks
- **Reduce human error**
- **Improve network uptime**
- **Scale IT operations without hiring**

> **Fact:** According to [Auvik’s Guide to Network Automation](https://www.auvik.com/franklyit/blog/network-automation/), even small teams can automate up to 70% of routine network management tasks.

## What Makes a Tool “Small Business Friendly”?

- Simple setup (no consultants required)
- Affordable pricing
- Easy to use, no coding or certifications needed
- Good support and documentation

![Laptop showing network dashboard, simple tools built for small teams](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1516321318423-f06f85e504b3?w=1200&h=600&fit=crop)

## Top Network Automation Tools for Small Businesses

### 1. Auvik
- **What it does:** Automated network monitoring, mapping, and configuration backup.
- **Why it’s great:** Cloud-based, easy onboarding, and designed for SMBs.
- [Learn more](https://www.auvik.com/franklyit/blog/network-automation/) _(external)_

### 2. NetBrain (Essentials)
- **What it does:** Visual network automation and troubleshooting.
- **Why it’s great:** Drag-and-drop automation, no scripting required.
- [Learn more](https://www.netbraintech.com/solutions/network-automation/) _(external)_

### 3. SolarWinds Network Configuration Manager (NCM)
- **What it does:** Automated config backups, compliance checks, and change management.
- **Why it’s great:** Scalable for small teams, strong support, and affordable entry-level plans.
- [Learn more](https://www.solarwinds.com/network-configuration-manager) _(external)_

### 4. ManageEngine Network Configuration Manager
- **What it does:** Multi-vendor config management and automation.
- **Why it’s great:** Intuitive UI, free edition for small networks.
- [Learn more](https://www.manageengine.com/network-configuration-manager/) _(external)_

### 5. Ubiquiti UniFi Network
- **What it does:** Automated device discovery, updates, and monitoring for UniFi hardware.
- **Why it’s great:** Perfect for small offices using Ubiquiti gear.
- [Learn more](https://ui.com/products/#unifi) _(external)_

## How to Choose the Right Tool

1. **List your top 3 pain points** (e.g., backups, device discovery, monitoring)
2. **Check compatibility** with your current hardware
3. **Start with a free trial or demo**
4. **Look for active support and community**

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Overbuying: Don’t pay for features you’ll never use
- Ignoring support: Choose vendors with responsive help
- Skipping training: Even simple tools need a quick onboarding

## Ready to Automate? Start Small, Win Big

You don’t need to be an enterprise to benefit from automation. Start with one tool, automate a single pain point, and build from there. Your future self (and your business) will thank you.
